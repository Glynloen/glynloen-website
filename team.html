Our team

The people behind the analysis.

A small but mighty team of experienced insurance professionals with a wide range of skills. We love what we do, and we look forward to deploying our talents on your behalf.

Some insurance matters run for several years, with long pauses for mediation, discovery disputes, and settlement conferences. GIC keeps the record it builds, with every citation intact, for the life of the engagement, so when a matter resumes or reaches testimony, the analysis behind an opinion never has to be reconstructed.

Principals

The people who lead every engagement.

Rick Bingham

Insurance Expert & GIC Principal

Rick brings over 44 years of experience in insurance, across claims, reinsurance, regulation, and run-off. He has examined more than 100 insurance carriers, and his experience spans claims handling & management, reinsurance claims, underwriting & binding, managing agency operations, billing & collections, staff training, regulatory compliance, and receivership. He has been recognized as a qualified expert in state and federal courts, and he has served on both the Hearing Board (14 years) and the Oversight Board of the Illinois Attorney Registration and Disciplinary Commission. Rick forms every opinion GIC renders, signs every report, and supports his work at deposition and trial.

RBingham@Glynloen.com

Dan Weber

Insurance Regulatory Attorney & Case-Mapping Specialist

Dan is an attorney and consulting expert who leads Glynloen’s case-mapping work, methodically constructing and exhaustively citing the record reviewed as a part of each engagement. He brings his legal background, information-security experience, and knowledge of emerging technologies to the analysis of large, complex document productions.

DWeber@Glynloen.com

The wider team

Depth across law, technology, and claims.

Jin Kong

Insurance Regulatory Attorney

Licensed in Ohio since 2013, Jin works as a federal government attorney advisor while maintaining a private practice. His focus areas include international business planning, emerging markets, data privacy & security, equity-based investments, non-profits, and globalization. He also brings a medical background and experience with due diligence, investigative reporting, and claims analysis. Jin has extensive experience in commercial insurance coverage disputes and claims-handling matters, advocating for both policyholders and insurers across property, liability, environmental pollution, inland marine, and cargo/logistics contexts.

JKong@Glynloen.com

Kathryn Marsan

IT Manager & Claims Specialist

Kathryn manages GIC’s information technology operations and brings claims examination experience across professional liability, nursing home, environmental, and homeowner lines. She conducts investigations, extracts financial data from loss run reports, and builds research packages using LexisNexis. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, magna cum laude, from the University of Cincinnati’s College of Applied Science.

KMarsan@Glynloen.com

Doug Weber

Analyst & Senior Researcher

Doug is an analyst with a background in insurance, regulatory compliance, and finance. A former insurance agent, he brings that experience to record review and analysis at Glynloen. He has conducted hundreds of compliance, due-diligence, and anti-money-laundering investigations, including matters submitted to the Treasury Department’s Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN), and holds an MBA from the Middlebury Institute of International Studies.

DMWeber@Glynloen.com

Lauren Campbell

Senior Consultant & Claims Specialist

Lauren has worked in insurance and risk management since 2021. She has performed state audits of insurance companies and conducted research and document review for bad-faith litigation and medical malpractice matters. She also works directly with insurers to organize and consult on comprehensive risk profiles, bringing strong organizational and observational skills to that work.

LCampbell@Glynloen.com

Karen Stobart

Paralegal & Technical Analyst

Previously with a prominent regional law firm, Karen designs custom data-management tools at GIC that enable complex data-analysis methodologies to be run across entire claim populations, giving clients detailed views of individual claims within larger datasets and generating additional valuable insights.

KStobart@Glynloen.com

Janelle Cahall

Litigation Support & Case Analytics Specialist

Janelle designs and runs GIC’s case-intake and document-processing workflows. She then assembles the firm’s analysis into a single indexed packet for expert review, giving Rick Bingham a verified, page-cited foundation for his further analysis and the forming of his opinions.
